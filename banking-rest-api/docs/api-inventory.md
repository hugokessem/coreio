# CoreIO API Inventory & Functional Mapping

This document provides a comprehensive inventory of all operations discovered in the CoreIO codebase, their inputs and outputs, underlying required PostgreSQL entities, and the corresponding NestJS REST endpoints.

| # | CoreIO Function | Behavior & Invariants | Input Parameters | Output Response | Required PostgreSQL Tables | New REST Endpoint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `AccountLookup` | Retrieves comprehensive account details including balances, currency, branch, category, customer profile, and restrictions. | `accountNumber`, `customerNumber` (optional) | Account metadata, working/available balance, branch info, KYC snapshot | `accounts`, `account_balances`, `customers`, `branches`, `currencies`, `account_types` | `POST /api/v1/accounts/lookup` |
| 2 | `AccountList` | Lists all accounts associated with a customer CIF or criteria. | `columnName` (e.g. `CUSTOMER`), `criteriaValue` | Array of account records with balances and statuses | `accounts`, `account_balances`, `customers`, `branches`, `currencies` | `GET /api/v1/accounts?customerId=...` |
| 3 | `AccountCreation` | Creates a new banking account for an existing customer with specified category, currency, and branch officer. | `customerNumber`, `category`, `currency`, `accountOfficer`, `productCode` | Newly provisioned account number, status, timestamps | `accounts`, `account_balances`, `customers`, `account_types`, `branches` | `POST /api/v1/accounts` |
| 4 | `AccountCreate` | Composite workflow that performs AML check, customer creation, and account opening in an orchestrated transaction. | Composite customer + account params | Customer details + Account details | `customers`, `accounts`, `account_balances`, `audit_logs` | `POST /api/v1/accounts/onboard` |
| 5 | `CustomerLookup` | Looks up customer identity and KYC demographics by customer identifier / number. | `customerIdentifier` | Name, DOB, gender, nationality, address, phone, email | `customers` | `POST /api/v1/customers/lookup` |
| 6 | `CustomerDetail` | Retrieves detailed customer profile including segment, branch code, and status. | `customerNumber` | Full customer profile and classification | `customers`, `branches` | `GET /api/v1/customers/:customerNumber` |
| 7 | `CustomerFetch` | Inquires customer and account list filtered by Account Number (`ACCT.ID`) or CIF (`CUS.ID`). | `fetchBy` (`ACCT.ID` \| `CUS.ID`), `customerNumber` | Customer info and linked accounts | `customers`, `accounts`, `account_balances` | `POST /api/v1/customers/fetch` |
| 8 | `CreateCustomer` | Enrolls a new customer with demographic, address, employment, tax (TIN), and legal ID information. | Demographics, address, employment, national ID, phone | Created customer ID, mnemonic, audit record | `customers` | `POST /api/v1/customers` |
| 9 | `CustomerDelete` | Soft-deletes or deactivates an unverified or pending customer record. | `customerNumber` | Deletion confirmation | `customers` | `DELETE /api/v1/customers/:customerNumber` |
| 10 | `ActionAutorization` | Approves and authorizes a pending/unauthorized customer record (maker-checker). | `customerNumber` | Authorized customer status | `customers`, `audit_logs` | `POST /api/v1/customers/:customerNumber/authorize` |
| 11 | `VerifyAML` | Performs Anti-Money Laundering (AML) check on a customer (FCM Status check). | `customerNumber` | `fcmStatus` (`PASS`, `FAIL`, `REVIEW`) | `customers`, `aml_records` | `POST /api/v1/customers/:customerNumber/verify-aml` |
| 12 | `AMLLookup` | Checks AML status associated with an account holder. | `accountNumber` | AML evaluation result | `accounts`, `customers`, `aml_records` | `POST /api/v1/accounts/:accountNumber/aml-lookup` |
| 13 | `PhoneLookup` | Looks up customer details and mnemonic by mobile phone number. | `phoneNumber` | `customerId`, `phoneNumber`, `email`, `fullName` | `customers` | `POST /api/v1/customers/phone-lookup` |
| 14 | `NameLookup` | Fast lookup of account title, owner name, CIF, currency, and restriction status for transfer validation. | `accountNumber` | Account title, customer name, currency, status | `accounts`, `customers` | `POST /api/v1/accounts/name-lookup` |
| 15 | `Fayda` | Verifies national citizen ID with Ethiopian Fayda National Identity service. | `nid` (National ID string) | `customerId`, `customerName`, `customerFlag` (`FOUND`/`NOT_FOUND`) | `customers`, `fayda_identities` | `POST /api/v1/identity/fayda/verify` |
| 16 | `CheckIfUserExists` | Pre-onboarding existence check: validates if phone number or national ID are already registered. | `phoneNumber`, `nationalId` | `success` (boolean), detail flags | `customers` | `POST /api/v1/customers/check-existence` |
| 17 | `Eligibility` | Evaluates if a customer or account is eligible for digital onboarding and mobile banking. | `fetchBy`, `criticalValue` | Eligibility status, restrictions, reason | `customers`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/customers/eligibility` |
| 18 | `FundTransferVerify` | Pre-flight validation and fee/commission calculation for fund transfer without posting balances. | Debit/credit account, amounts, currency, service code | Estimated fees, taxes, disaster fund, net debit/credit | `accounts`, `account_balances`, `services`, `service_limits` | `POST /api/v1/transfers/verify` |
| 19 | `FundTransfer` | Atomically executes debit and credit transfer with double-entry ledger postings, pessimistic row locking, and fees. | Debit/credit account, amount, currency, ref, channel | Generated `ftNumber`, balances after, ledger status | `accounts`, `account_balances`, `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `idempotency_keys` | `POST /api/v1/transfers` |
| 20 | `FundTransferCheck` | Checks the execution status and details of a posted fund transfer by its FT reference number. | `ftNumber` | Transaction details, ledger status, timestamp | `transactions`, `ledger_entries` | `GET /api/v1/transfers/:ftNumber` |
| 21 | `StatusCheck` | Queries transaction status by client reference / transaction ID (`MTOREF`). | `transactionId` | Transfer status, amounts, accounts, channel | `transactions` | `GET /api/v1/transfers/status/:transactionId` |
| 22 | `RevertFundTransfer` | Performs an authorized reversal of a previously posted financial transaction with compensating ledger postings. | `transactionId` | Reversal FT number, compensating postings | `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `account_balances` | `POST /api/v1/transfers/revert` |
| 23 | `CreateLockedAmount` | Places an administrative, security, or transaction hold on an account's funds. | `accountNumber`, `amount`, `description`, `from`, `to` | Generated `lockId`, held amount, expiration | `accounts`, `account_balances`, `account_holds` | `POST /api/v1/accounts/holds` |
| 24 | `ListLockedAmount` | Lists all active and historical locked amounts (holds) on an account. | `accountNumber` | Array of active holds (lockId, amount, remark) | `account_holds`, `accounts` | `GET /api/v1/accounts/:accountNumber/holds` |
| 25 | `ReleaseLockedAmount` | Releases an existing active hold, returning funds to the account's available balance. | `transactionId` (lockId) | Released hold details, released timestamp | `account_holds`, `account_balances` | `POST /api/v1/accounts/holds/release` |
| 26 | `LockedAmountFT` | Executes a fund transfer directly utilizing previously locked/held funds and releasing the hold. | Debit/credit account, `lockId`, amount, service code | Completed FT number, released lock details | `account_holds`, `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `account_balances` | `POST /api/v1/transfers/from-hold` |
| 27 | `CreateStandingOrder` | Registers a recurring scheduled transfer between accounts with interval frequency and duration. | Debit/credit account, amount, frequency, date range | Created `orderId`, next execution date, status | `standing_orders`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/standing-orders` |
| 28 | `ListStandingOrder` | Lists all standing orders configured for a given debit account. | `accountNumber` | Array of standing order definitions | `standing_orders` | `GET /api/v1/standing-orders?accountNumber=...` |
| 29 | `ListStandingOrderHistory` | Retrieves execution log history for an account's standing orders. | `accountNumber` | Historical execution timestamps and amounts | `standing_orders`, `transactions` | `GET /api/v1/standing-orders/history` |
| 30 | `UpdateStandingOrder` | Modifies parameters (amount, frequency, end date) of an existing standing order. | `orderId`, amount, frequency, endDate, accounts | Updated standing order | `standing_orders` | `PUT /api/v1/standing-orders/:orderId` |
| 31 | `CancleStandingOrder` | Cancels and terminates an active standing order. | `accountNumber`, `orderId` | Cancellation confirmation | `standing_orders` | `DELETE /api/v1/standing-orders/:orderId` |
| 32 | `MiniStatementByLimit` | Retrieves the most recent N transactions posted to an account with details and counterparty. | `accountNumber`, `numberOfTransaction` | Array of recent statement records | `ledger_entries`, `transactions`, `accounts` | `GET /api/v1/accounts/:accountNumber/statement?limit=...` |
| 33 | `MiniStatementByDateRange` | Retrieves account statement entries within a specific date range with opening and closing balances. | `accountNumber`, `from`, `to` | Statement records, starting/ending balances | `ledger_entries`, `transactions`, `accounts` | `GET /api/v1/accounts/:accountNumber/statement?from=...&to=...` |
| 34 | `ExchangeRates` | Retrieves current bank foreign exchange rates (Buy, Sell, Mid) for supported currencies against ETB. | None | Array of currency rates (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.) | `exchange_rates`, `currencies` | `GET /api/v1/exchange-rates` |
| 35 | `ServiceDetail` | Retrieves configured banking service parameters, charge codes, and limits. | `serviceCode` (optional) | Array of service details, charges, max amounts | `services` | `GET /api/v1/services` |
| 36 | `ServiceLimit` | Inquires or updates channel-specific service limits (max transaction amount, daily count). | Channel limits definition | Configured service limits | `service_limits`, `services` | `GET /api/v1/services/limits` |
| 37 | `CustomerLimitFetchByCIF` | Fetches custom service and channel transaction limits assigned to a customer CIF. | `customerNumber` | Custom customer channel and service limits | `customer_limits`, `customers` | `GET /api/v1/customers/:customerNumber/limits` |
| 38 | `CustomerLimitAmendByCIF` | Amends custom transaction limits for a customer CIF. | `customerNumber`, `channelLimit` | Updated customer limits | `customer_limits` | `PUT /api/v1/customers/:customerNumber/limits` |
| 39 | `BillPayment` | Processes a utility or corporate bill payment transaction. | Debit account, bill ref, service code, amount | Bill payment receipt, FT reference | `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/payments/bill` |
| 40 | `BillPaymentVerify` | Pre-validates bill payment parameters and checks billing amounts. | Debit account, bill ref, amount, service code | Verification result | `accounts`, `services` | `POST /api/v1/payments/bill/verify` |
| 41 | `SplitPayment` | Executes a 1-to-many payment debiting one account and crediting multiple destination accounts atomically. | Debit account, array of credit accounts & amounts | Master transaction ref, breakdown entries | `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/payments/split` |
| 42 | `RTGS` | Initiates Real-Time Gross Settlement large-value interbank payment (e.g. Ministry of Revenue taxes). | Debit account, beneficiary bank, bill ID, amount | RTGS FT reference, processing status | `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/payments/rtgs` |
| 43 | `CardRequest` | Orders a new debit or ATM payment card for an account. | `accountNumber`, `branchCode`, `phoneNumber`, `cardType` | Card request reference, order status | `card_requests`, `accounts`, `branches` | `POST /api/v1/cards/request` |
| 44 | `CardReplace` | Requests replacement of an ATM/debit card with specified reason (damaged, lost, stolen). | `accountNumber`, `cardType`, `replacementReason` | Card replacement confirmation | `card_requests`, `cards` | `POST /api/v1/cards/replace` |
| 45 | `SuperAppSubscribe` | Enrolls or registers a SuperApp user code and links to customer. | `userCode`, `customerId`, `channel`, `phoneNumber` | Subscription record | `super_app_users`, `customers` | `POST /api/v1/superapp/subscribe` |
| 46 | `SuperAppUnsubscribe` | Cancels a SuperApp user's channel registration. | `userCode` | Unsubscription confirmation | `super_app_users` | `POST /api/v1/superapp/unsubscribe` |
| 47 | `SuperAppStatusChange` | Changes status of a SuperApp user (e.g. `ACTIVE`, `BLOCKED`, `SUSPENDED`). | `userCode`, `status` | Updated user record | `super_app_users` | `PUT /api/v1/superapp/status` |
| 48 | `IPS:AccountLookup` | Performs inter-bank account verification via EthSwitch/IPS network. | `debitBankBIC`, `creditBankBIC`, `creditAccountNumber` | Interbank verification result, account holder name | `institutions`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/ips/account-lookup` |
| 49 | `IPS:FundTransfer` | Executes an inter-bank fund transfer via EthSwitch/IPS ISO 20022 clearing. | Interbank transfer parameters, BIC, amounts | IPS clearing response, settlement ID | `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `institutions` | `POST /api/v1/ips/transfer` |
| 50 | `IPS:StatusCheck` | Inquires status of an inter-bank payment via EthSwitch/IPS pacs.028 message. | `debitBankBIC`, `originalTransactionId` | Interbank settlement status (`ACSC`, etc.) | `transactions`, `institutions` | `POST /api/v1/ips/status-check` |
| 51 | `IPS:QRPayment` | Processes a merchant QR code payment via EthSwitch/IPS standard. | QR payload, merchant ID, amount, accounts | QR payment settlement confirmation | `transactions`, `ledger_entries` | `POST /api/v1/ips/qr-payment` |
| 52 | `Wallet:CustomerAccountLookup` | Inquires customer identity and KYC from phone-linked digital wallet (CBE Birr). | `phoneNumber` | KYC name, status, mobile account | `customers`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/wallet/customer-lookup` |
| 53 | `Wallet:CustomerFundTransfer` | Transfers money between bank account and mobile wallet (Bank-to-Wallet / Wallet-to-Bank). | `ftNumber`, `primaryParty` (shortcode), `receiverParty` (phone), `amount` | Transaction receipt and confirmation | `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/wallet/transfer` |
| 54 | `Wallet:AgentAccountLookup` | Inquires wallet agent status and credentials by agent code/phone. | `phoneNumber` (agent code) | Agent verification result | `customers`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/wallet/agent-lookup` |
| 55 | `Wallet:AgentFundTransfer` | Processes agent-assisted wallet cash-in/cash-out or float transfer. | Agent transfer parameters | Float transfer receipt | `transactions`, `ledger_entries`, `accounts` | `POST /api/v1/wallet/agent-transfer` |
